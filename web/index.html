Embodied conducting & spatial orchestration learning

Learn how gesture, timing and placement shape an orchestra.

Conduct a virtual ensemble with your hand in front of the webcam and get live feedback on beat, tempo and pattern shape. Then arrange instruments on a 3D stage and hear how position changes what reaches the listener.

Conductor

Conduct a 4/4 pattern. Beat detection, tempo estimation and DTW pattern comparison run locally.

  • Webcam (MediaPipe Hands)
  • Mouse / trackpad pad
  • Recorded test trajectories
  • WebXR controller (headset required)

Composer

  • Volume, mute, solo, articulation
  • Listener at podium or in the audience
  • Save, load and A/B compare arrangements
